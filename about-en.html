ABOUT // FRUSTRATOR

Frustrator is a place to rest from control and from chasing goals. Here, frustration is not burnout or helplessness — it is relaxation and acceptance. The app invites you to let go of the need to control and to become an observer or a partner instead.

Your actions may change something, or they may not. Sometimes consequences arrive with a delay — and by then it's unclear what caused them. That's not a bug. That's the point.

If you do nothing at all, you can still enjoy the music. Just let Frustrator do its thing on its own. That option is neither better nor worse than any other. No one here will judge or evaluate you.

technical side

The app consists of six audio modules, each with six to eleven adjustable parameters. When launched, every parameter is set to a random value. By moving smoke rings across the screen, you blindly redistribute these values — some go up, some go down. Each time you open the app, the zones responsible for different module parameters are reshuffled and assigned new random values. You can just explore — that's enjoyable too.

ABOUT // FRUSTRATOR

Frustrator is a tool for taking a break from control, a tool for frustration, and a temporary escape from chasing endless goals. Here, frustration is viewed not as burnout or helplessness, but as relaxation and pure acceptance. The app invites you to give up trying to control everything and instead become an observer or a partner.

Your actions may lead to consequences, or they may not. Or the consequences will arrive much later, leaving it completely unclear what exactly caused them. The faster the feeling of frustration comes, the faster you can begin to enjoy the process. No one is going to judge or evaluate you.

If you are interested in the technical side, the app consists of 6 audio modules, each with 6 to 11 adjustable parameters. When launched, every parameter is set to a randomized value. By moving smoke rings across the screen, you blindly redistribute these values — increasing them in some areas and decreasing them in others. Each time you open the app, the zones responsible for different module parameters are scrambled and assigned new random values. Everything is designed for the pure joy of exploration.

But even if you choose to do absolutely nothing, you can still just enjoy the music. This is another way to interact — doing nothing at all and letting Frustrator do its thing completely on its own. This option is neither better nor worse.
